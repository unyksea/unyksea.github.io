# 2026–2027 scheduling notes

Internal reference for the committee. Not published to the site.

Every date below was taken from the institution's own registrar or academic-affairs
page in August 2026. Re-verify before committing to a venue — calendars get revised.

## Why scheduling is hard here

The seven institutions with chapter members do not share a spring break. There is
**no week in March when all seven are in session**:

| Institution | Spring 2027 break |
| --- | --- |
| RPI | Feb 27 – Mar 7 (earliest) |
| RIT | Mar 7 – 14 |
| University of Rochester | Mar 6 – 14 |
| Binghamton | Mar 6 – 14 |
| Syracuse | Mar 7 – 14 |
| University at Buffalo | Mar 15 – 20 |
| Cornell | Mar 27 – Apr 4 (latest) |

**April 5 onward is the first window clear for all seven**, and it stays clear until
finals begin Apr 28 (RIT, Syracuse). That three-week window is where the spring's
major events belong.

## Lower-confidence items

- **RPI spring dates** came from the Student Living & Learning office page, not the
  registrar (whose calendar page rendered only Fall 2026). RPI's commencement date
  could not be confirmed from any accessible source; only residence-hall move-out
  (May 8) was found. Confirm with the RPI registrar before scheduling anything in
  the Capital Region in May.
- **Binghamton's fall break (Oct 10–18)** is unusually long. Two Binghamton pages
  agree, but worth a second look.
