**Saturday, September 12, 2026 at 4:00 pm — Snee Hall 2146, Cornell University.**

The first of two welcoming socials that open the 2026–2027 program year, serving the **eastern hub** of the chapter — Cornell, Binghamton, RPI, and Syracuse.

## Format

- An **academic seminar** for students and researchers: a 45–50 minute talk followed by open Q&A and discussion
- An introduction to **KSEA membership** and what the chapter does
- **Ice-breaking and introductions** — the point of the evening is that people leave knowing each other

New students and postdocs who have just arrived in the region are especially welcome. You do not need to be a member to attend.
