The chapter opened its 2026–2027 program year at **245 Olin Hall, Cornell University** on Saturday, September 12, 2026 — the first of two welcoming socials, serving the **eastern hub**: Cornell, Binghamton, RPI, and Syracuse.

## Programme

| Time | |
| --- | --- |
| **4:00 – 5:00 pm** | Introduction to KSEA, and an icebreaking session |
| **5:00 – 6:30 pm** | Networking dinner |

The session introduced KSEA and what the chapter does, followed by introductions over dinner — the point of the evening being that people left knowing each other.

The western counterpart followed a week later at RIT. [See the Western Hub social →](/events/2026-09-welcoming-social-western)
