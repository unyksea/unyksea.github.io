**Saturday, September 12, 2026 · 4:00 – 6:30 pm · 245 Olin Hall, Cornell University**

The first of two welcoming socials that open the 2026–2027 program year, serving the **eastern hub** of the chapter — Cornell, Binghamton, RPI, and Syracuse.

## Schedule

| Time | |
| --- | --- |
| **4:00 – 5:00 pm** | Introduction to KSEA, and an icebreaking session |
| **5:00 – 6:30 pm** | Networking dinner |

## RSVP required

Dinner is catered, so we need a headcount in advance. **Please [RSVP here](https://forms.gle/SCdm5j8CNfoeKFze7)** — it takes a minute, and it is what makes sure there is food for you.

## Getting there and parking

The venue is **245 Olin Hall**, on Campus Road. Three parking lots are within a short walk, marked in green on the map above:

- **Lot 1** — directly east of Olin Hall on Campus Road, the closest option
- **Lot 2** — northwest, near Willard Straight Hall and the Cornell Store
- **Lot 3** — southwest, along College Avenue near Cornell Law School

Parking on the Cornell campus is generally free on weekends, but check the posted signs at the lot you use.

New students and postdocs who have just arrived in the region are especially welcome. **You do not need to be a member to attend** — come find out what the chapter does.
