**Saturday, November 14, 2026 · from 2:00 pm · Syracuse University**

The chapter's largest gathering of the fall, and a deliberate change of shape from a year-in-review meeting: an afternoon built around **careers and wellbeing**, with three hours of sessions before dinner.

Syracuse is roughly central to the region, so the Forum brings the eastern and western hubs together in one room.

## Program

| Time | Session |
| --- | --- |
| **2:00 – 3:00 pm** | **Mental health clinic** |
| **3:00 – 4:00 pm** | **Unconventional career paths** — 변유정, Ph.D., New York State Department of Health |
| **4:00 – 5:00 pm** | **Career-stage workshops** — three parallel tracks |
| **5:00 – 7:00 pm** | **Networking dinner** |

### Unconventional career paths

**변유정, Ph.D.**, currently with the **New York State Department of Health**, on building a career outside the traditional academic track:

- Career opportunities in state government for Korean women scientists
- Navigating a professional career while raising a family
- Work–life balance in practice

### Career-stage workshops

Three parallel sessions, so everyone gets something pitched at their own stage:

| For | Session |
| --- | --- |
| **Undergraduates** | The MD–PhD pathway |
| **Graduate students and postdocs** | Fellowship Writing 101 |
| **Faculty** | Building a successful career in academia |

## Dinner

A **networking dinner** follows from around 5:00 pm — the main reason the Forum is worth the drive, and the best opportunity of the year to meet members from other institutions.
