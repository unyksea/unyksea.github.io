The Year-End Forum closes out the fall program and sets up the spring.

## Program

- **Networking** across the full chapter membership
- **Highlights of the year's achievements** — what the chapter and its members accomplished
- **Recognition of active members** who contributed their time
- **Discussion of plans** for the remainder of the program year

This is the fall's flagship event and the best single opportunity to meet members from institutions other than your own.
