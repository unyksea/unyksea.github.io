**Saturday, November 14, 2026 · 1:00 – 7:00 pm · [Hall of Languages, Syracuse University](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hall+of+Languages%2C+Syracuse+University), Rooms 107, 114 and 115**

The chapter's largest gathering of the fall, for Korean-American scholars and professionals at every career stage: two featured talks, breakout sessions pitched at your own career stage, and a networking dinner.

Syracuse is roughly central to the region, so the workshop brings the eastern and western hubs together in one room.

**[Register by Sunday, November 8](https://forms.gle/jtWvYVGmsJBcNdeN6)** — registration is required so we can plan the dinner.
