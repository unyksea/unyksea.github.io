The Annual Symposium is the chapter's flagship event of the year, bringing together researchers, professionals, and students from across Upstate New York to present their work.

## Program

- **Keynote talks** from invited speakers
- A **poster session** for graduate and undergraduate researchers
- **Interdisciplinary discussion** across fields that rarely share a room
- **Networking and mentorship** opportunities for students and early-career researchers

Building on the 2025 symposium at RIT and the 2026 spring symposium at the University of Rochester Medical Center, the 2027 edition is planned as the largest yet — roughly 60 attendees.

Calls for talks and posters will be posted here. [Let us know](/contact) if you would like to present or help organize.
