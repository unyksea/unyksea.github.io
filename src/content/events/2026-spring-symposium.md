The chapter hosted its 2026 Spring Symposium on **March 21, 2026** at the University of Rochester Medical Center, gathering researchers, professionals, and students from across the region under the theme *Building the Future in the Age of AI*.

The symposium reflected how far the chapter has come. In a little over a year it grew **from a group of about 30 to 71 members** — a network of Korean-American scientists and engineers spanning every major research institution in Upstate New York.

## Invited talks

**Prof. Sam Chung** — Dean, School of Technology & Computing, City University of Seattle
*How AI Impacts the Future Job Market.* A look at how AI is reshaping career opportunities and shifting the ground under entire industries.

**Prof. Deok-Ho Kim** — Biomedical Engineering, Johns Hopkins University
*Advanced Human Microphysiological Systems for Disease Modeling, Drug Development, and Space Biology.*

**Prof. Ohbong J. Kwon** — Computer Engineering Technology, New York City College of Technology
*Teaching Computer Engineering in the Age of AI.*

## The first student poster session

To draw students further into the chapter, the organizing committee introduced its **first-ever poster session**. Undergraduate and graduate students presented their research, took feedback from faculty and postdoctoral mentors, and built professional connections outside their own departments. Cash prizes recognized the strongest work.
