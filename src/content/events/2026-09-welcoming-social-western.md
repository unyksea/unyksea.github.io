The second welcoming social of the year, serving the **western hub** — RIT, the University of Rochester, and the University at Buffalo.

## Format

- An **academic seminar** for students and researchers: a 45–50 minute talk followed by open Q&A and discussion
- An introduction to **KSEA membership** and what the chapter does
- **Ice-breaking and introductions** for newcomers to the region

Running the opening event twice, once per hub, is deliberate: Upstate New York is too large for a single social to serve everyone.
