The second of two welcoming socials opening the 2026–2027 program year, held on **Saturday, September 19, 2026** at **[1140 Institute Hall, Rochester Institute of Technology](https://maps.app.goo.gl/U7VdiqzfJsVHDakw5)**, serving the **western hub** — RIT, the University of Rochester, and the University at Buffalo.

Running the opening social twice, once per hub, is deliberate: Upstate New York is too large for a single social to serve everyone.

The afternoon introduced KSEA and the chapter's programme for the year, followed by time for members across the region's western institutions to meet each other.
