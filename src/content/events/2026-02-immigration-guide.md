**이공계 전공자를 위한 H-1B 및 영주권 핵심 가이드**
*(A guide to H-1B and permanent residency for STEM professionals)*

Presented online by **Ui Jun Suk, J.D.** of US Law Group on February 6, 2026.

Immigration status is one of the most consistently requested topics from chapter members — this session walked through the H-1B and green card processes as they apply specifically to researchers and engineers.
