The chapter opened its 2025–2026 program year with a fall symposium at Cornell University's College of Veterinary Medicine on **October 4, 2025**.

## Program

- **Trainee research seminar** — graduate students and postdocs presenting their current work to a cross-disciplinary audience.
- **Career development seminar** — navigating the academic and industry paths open to Korean-American researchers in the U.S.
- **Panel discussion** — an open conversation between faculty, industry professionals, and trainees.
