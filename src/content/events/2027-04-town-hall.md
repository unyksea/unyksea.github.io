The annual town hall is the chapter's one formal governance meeting of the year, and it is open to every member.

## Agenda

- **Discussion of next year's events** and the direction of the chapter
- **Election of the local chapter president** and the incoming committee
- **Chapter transition meeting** — handover from the outgoing officers

If you have thoughts on what the chapter should do differently, this is the meeting where that gets decided.
